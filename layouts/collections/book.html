How this text was read

The text was read from the scan by a vision language model, a kind of AI that reads page images, and then checked page by page by machine. The error rate is low, but it is never zero. Until people have proofread every page against the scan, expect occasional slips: a dropped accent, a misread foreign word, a running head left in the text, a paragraph split where a page turned, a letter’s date or heading set in the wrong place.
