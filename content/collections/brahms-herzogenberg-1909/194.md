      "t": ",—Was it of your own devising or by a lucky chance that your letter arrived on Christmas Eve, the first and most precious of Christmas presents? It meant much to me to read my name in your dear writing again at last, and if you would take that to heart you might be less chary of setting pen to paper. It is a pity, for you used not to be so lazy; indeed, you honoured me with many a nice long letter. However, I am well satisfied with the contents of this one, and can hardly fail to agree with your proposal to send me music now and then ‘without writing,’ while I find ‘nice things’ to say in reply. Please do not forget this delightful compact, but act upon it soon!"
