      "t": "—Your sad account of poor Holstein was so unexpected that I was as much shocked as pained. I find it hopeless to write to him, or even to her. Were I in similar case, I should not expect it from my best friend. Yet I know that women demand such consolation; and I therefore ask you to act as my interpreter for the moment. She is bound to feel your departure very keenly, poor thing! Personal sympathy is the greatest comfort after all, and a real help; but writing to inquire for news, when you know it can only be bad and have no hope of improvement, is tragic. . . . I seem to remember your telling me you were going to stay with Dr. Oberhofer in Vienna ? I met him in Rome and was reminded of it."
