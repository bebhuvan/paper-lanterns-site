For you know (though I can’t resist repeating it) that your music is as indispensable to our existence as air, light, and heat. You can’t think how glad we are not to have to give the dead masters all our affection and enthusiasm, and how glad that the one to whom we already owe so much still lives and labours, and is, we hope, neither inaccessible nor quite indifferent to us.
