For the first three-quarters there was not a sound from the other room, and I thought they were merely preparing for the real horror, when suddenly the door opened (at 9.30), and the doctors brought him back all neatly bandaged. I had a terrible fright, thinking something dreadful must have happened to interrupt the operation. However, thank God, it was all happily over, and Heinrich came to himself again gradually in bed. When he asked for our doctor, who had left the room for a moment, and I told him how glad he was that it had all gone so well and so quickly, the poor fellow exclaimed, ‘What, is it all over?’ and on receiving my assurance he burst into tears of joy.
