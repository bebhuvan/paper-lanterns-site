You don’t realize how important it is not to avoid the obvious too scrupulously. Life would be greatly impoverished if everyone practised this saving plan, already adopted by so many! I will not expatiate on the beauty of the neighbourhood, as I am bad at descriptions, but will only say I never saw anything to equal it. It might be stage scenery for Gluck's operas!
