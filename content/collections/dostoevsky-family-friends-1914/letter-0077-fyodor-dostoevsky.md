That applies especially to Russia, for with us national consciousness is based on Christianity. “ A Christian peasant-people ”; “ believing Russia ”: these are our fundamental conceptions. The essential problem of our day is : How are we to persuade our educated classes of this principle ? If one but utters a word in such a sense, one will either be devoured alive, or denounced as a traitor. And whom shall one have betrayed ?
