We had scarcely, however, crossed the Bunas ourselves, when we found the whole line halted: it being undetermined whether the camp was to be pitched again, or whether the army was to proceed further on. After a full hour's delay, the line moved on, and we marched about six miles; when another halt took place, and fresh doubts were started as to where the camp was intended to be pitched. At this moment Balo Rao, with a numerous train of attendants, passed by, to rejoin his detachment before Doonee; and everybody followed him.
