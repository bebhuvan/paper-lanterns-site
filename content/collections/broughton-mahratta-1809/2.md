These Hirkarus are a most active and useful set of men : their business is to obtain information, to act as guides, and convey letters or messages : they are always well acquainted with the country, its roads, passes through hills, or across rivers ; and are so generally useful, that, like the ancient heralds, they are looked upon as privileged, and allowed to pass unmolested where other travellers would be in the utmost danger. They are often sent to great distances, and are absent for many months ; when they must necessarily be subjected to the greatest hardships, in a country where there are no inns, and where the inhabitants of many provinces subsist entirely by plunder."
