Our first march was on the 2nd inst. to Deepree, a village upon the right bank of the Chumbul. Its distance from Kutolee is not more than thirteen miles; but Zalim Sing,h knew the Mahrattas too well to let them cross the river at that place, where the ford is a tolerably good one, and pass through the skirts of the town. We were obliged, therefore, to march four miles along the bank of the Parbuttee to gain another ford, and a most abominable one it was, at a place called Balohpa. The bed of the river is a mass of large fragments of rock; and the banks, especially the left one, are very steep and difficult of ascent."
