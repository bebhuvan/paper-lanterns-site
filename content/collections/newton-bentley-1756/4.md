And tho’ Gravity might give the Planets a Motion of Deſcent towards the Sun, either directly or with ſome little Obliquity, yet the tranſverſe Motions by which they revolve in their ſeveral Orbs, required the divine Arm to impreſs them according to the Tangents of their Orbs. I would now add, that the Hypotheſis of Matter’s being at firſt evenly ſpread through the Heavens, is, in my Opinion, inconſiſtent with the Hypotheſis of innate Gravity, without a ſupernatural Power to reconcile them, and therefore it infers a Deity.
