Accordingly, distracted as I am by my wife's ill-health, the dangerous sickness of some of my servants, and the death of others, I fly to my studies, those sovereign composers of my grief. It is true, they give me a keener perception of misfortunes, but they teach me too how to bear them more patiently. It is an established rule with me, before I publish anything, to take the judgement of my friends upon it, especially yours.
