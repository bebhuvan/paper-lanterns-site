As for me, after lying on my back three weeks, I find that I have been shipped by Tedium into the region of Doldrums, where all things droop, and Patience, like a trodden Toad, hops and yawns in the endeavour to act up to her name, under whip of Necessity. The bone seems to be mending. To-morrow a man comes to examine it by X rays. If favourable, I may hope to have the leg in plaster of Paris and say good-bye to bed by day. Had you seen the leg at first, you would have conceived a blackamoor emerging from a prize-fight. Now it is like Tommy after settling accounts at school.
