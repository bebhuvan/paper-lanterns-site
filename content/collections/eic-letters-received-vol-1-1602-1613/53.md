Your letter, with a sword blade, I have received, returning you many thanks for your kindness and will be ready to requite your courtesy upon any occasion if your Worship make use of me, whereof I pray doubt not. What hath been sent both unto your Worship and people ashore was not with expectation of recompence, but in token of good will partly to manifest my love I bear you. I doubt not but within this eight days answer will come from the Basha with order for your people's deliverance.
