Your red prayer book likewise is gone, we have made enquiry for it with promise of recompense to him that brings news of it, but as yet we cannot understand what is become of it. The last night was a very extraordinary night, nothing but preparations of soldiers and weapons with talking of boats, we expected the attempting of some great matter, but we see nothing that passed.
