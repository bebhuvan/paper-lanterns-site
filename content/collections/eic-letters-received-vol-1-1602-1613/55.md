Likewise I have sent you herewith in token of good will, two oxen, 5 bags of tame and 5 rowels of rice, which I pray you take in good part. The time runneth on apace, and we very shortly expect news from Zenan, for the delivery of your people, who, we hope, shall be shortly delivered.
