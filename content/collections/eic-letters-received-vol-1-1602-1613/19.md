The pack of cloth I have received, with refreshing, for the which I most humbly thank you for. Tobacco I have none, but such pipes as I could get I have sent, not having any myself. John Frances is fast in the bilboes, and with a pair of manacles, and according to your order otherwise concerning him, I will, and it shall be effected.
