The dramatic poet is also a historian, and he can no more alter history than human nature. Now the thirteenth century is a period of twilight; it has deep shadows, very little light, violence, crime, innumerable superstitions, and great barbarousness, everywhere. The Jews were barbarous, so were the Christians; the Christians were the oppressors, the Jews were the oppressed; the Jews retaliated. What else could be expected? It is the natural action of every compressed spring and oppressed people. The Jews therefore revenged themselves in secret; legend or history, the story of the little Saint-Werner child proves it.
