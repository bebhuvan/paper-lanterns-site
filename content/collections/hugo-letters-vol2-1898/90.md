I replied: “It does not rest with me; I shall be expelled.” “Wait till you are expelled,” was the reply. I said to them: “But if we make a scandal of it, which may be a useful political step, there will be joint responsibility; you will, perhaps, all be expelled.” “Well, we will follow you, and rally round you again in Jersey.
