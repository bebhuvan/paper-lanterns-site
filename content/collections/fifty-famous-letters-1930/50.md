First, I have to thank the Almighty for having preserved my own sweet and adored husband, and for the blessing of having him by me, so dear, so precious, during my confinement. After three days he had to go, and when he got near Aschaffenburg found fighting going on. We could hear the guns near. The Prussians shot from the roofs of the houses; they fought in the streets; it must have been horrid. Our troops retreated ( as had always been intended ) in perfect order.
