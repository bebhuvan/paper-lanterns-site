For my part, Lord Ochus, I have never yet known how to command, and would no more undertake such a task, than pretend to play at dice without ever being taught. If others were of the same mind, we should doubtless have fewer troubles than we have, whereas now the confidence of those that are ignorant exceeds the ability of such as are skillful. Hence it comes that fortune is made yet greater than she really is whose folly increases her power.
