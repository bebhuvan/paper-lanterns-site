I scowled at each and every one, for I was ready, my mouth wide open, to fling myself on the pudding itself. Then they started wasting more time; they would nibble a nut and pass the wine round again just to make more delay. It seemed indeed as if they were holding up my appetite on purpose. One of them took a toothpick and began cleaning his teeth: another threw himself back on the couch and apparently thought more of going to sleep than of eating his dinner. The others meanwhile chatted one to the other, doing anything except proceed to the enjoyment of that dear delicious pudding. At last some kind saint, I imagine, took pity on my parched throat, and seeing how I longed for it, allowed me to satisfy my desires.
