But when the gods wish to save a man they pluck him even from the edge of the abyss, and so it was they snatched me from the jaws of the three-headed dog who guards, men say, the gates of hell. That terrible old man had scarcely had time to bring my case before the Council, when he was seized with a fever and in three days was dead. He is lying now stark and cold and his people are preparing for the funeral, while I ran off as fast as my legs could carry me. Now I am safe.
