What a lot, what a lot you are missing! The feast of the Threshing-floor, the feast of the Tribesmen, the feast of the Wine-god, and grandest of all the feast of the Harvest Queen, which we are now celebrating. The first day—the Return—is over: this morning at Athens it is the Fasting: to-morrow is the Sacrifice to the Fair-Mother. I beg you for my sake and my brothers'. Heaven forbid that you should end your days without having had a taste of town pleasures. It would be dreadful: no better than an animal's life.
