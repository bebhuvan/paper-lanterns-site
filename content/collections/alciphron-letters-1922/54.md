      "t": "You know Timon, Leafson, the son of Echecratides of Collytus. When he was rich he squandered his substance on us parasites and on courtesans, and so he was brought to penury. Then he laid aside his genial ways and became a misanthrope hating all men, like the fellow in the story who had never known pain. He lives now on his upland farm and pelts the passers-by with clods of earth. His only desire is that no man shall ever have intercourse with him again. He loathes and abhors our common humanity. As for the rest of the new made millionaires at Athens, they are meaner than anything, worse than Stingy or Grasper even. The time has come for me to migrate from town and earn my living by hard work. Please take me then on your farm as a hired labourer : I am prepared to endure any hardship if only I can fill my insatiate belly."
