The greater part of all my intellectual work is done in the virtual solitude in which we live here, and the time which is not taken up in writing (in which at present both of us are occupied) we spend in wandering alone about the mountains and wilds of this part of France, gathering the health and spirits which are necessary to render life in England endurable to us.
