Lord Byron, as Shelley's executor, had made application to him for an allowance for his son's widow and child. — “I must decline all interference with matters in which Mrs. Shelley is interested. As to the child, I am inclined to afford the means of a suitable protection and care of him in this country if he shall be placed with a person I shall approve.” On receipt of this information from Lord Byron Mrs. Shelley wrote — “He does not offer him an asylum in his own house, but a beggarly provision under the care of a stranger!
