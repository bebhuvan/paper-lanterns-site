Won’t you do something to show that you are in any way removed from the common run of men?—Only indulging in madness ! . . .To-day you have your bell, to-morrow you add a horn, and follow suit with a chowry the day after; or you introduce a cot to-day, and to-morrow you have its legs silver-mounted, and people help themselves to a rice-porridge, and you spin out two thousand cock-and-bull stories—in short, nothing but external ceremonies. This is called in English Imbecility. Those into whose heads nothing but that sort of silliness enters, are called imbecile.
