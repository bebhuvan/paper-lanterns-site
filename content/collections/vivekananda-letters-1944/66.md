Instead of materialising the spirit, i.e., dragging the spiritual to the material plane as these folks do, convert the matter into spirit, catch a glimpe at least, every day, of that world of infinite beauty and peace and purity—the spiritual, and try to live in it day and night. Seek not, touch not with your toes even, anything that is uncanny.
