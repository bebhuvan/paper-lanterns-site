The King requesteth that you would not make known that any lead was to be had here in Firando, because he intendeth to write himself in like manner to Saffiandono and would not be taken false in his writing, for fear of displeasure. Now if you have sold all, they may as well take it of such merchants as you have sold it to as of us; but if you have not before the receipt hereof, I pray you furnish the Emperor, for we had much to do to keep our lead here although bargained for to another."
