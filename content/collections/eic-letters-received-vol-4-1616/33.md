Also it is thought that General Keeling will come likewise in one of his ships from Seratt to this place, who is to stay as General in the Indies five years and is to come hither to Japon and to go to visit all the rest of the factories in the Indies. Captain Downton made a great fight likewise at Seratt with the Portigese, having burnt three of the Portigese galleons ; besides did kill a number of men with loss of some three or four Englishmen.
