And now I gladly recognize that yours is the hand to crown the edifice of our long mutual regard by this most timely endowment of the church in our poor town of Clermont, whose unworthy bishop I am. Under your late sister's will, you were only a co-heir, but the example of your piety has already moved your surviving sister to emulate your good works.
