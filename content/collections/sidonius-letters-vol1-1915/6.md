Have done ! awake ! sleek ease has unstrung the sinews of your mind ; raise it to higher things. Is it a less duty in a man of your descent to cultivate himself than his estate ? In fine, what you are pleased to call a young man's exercise is really a relaxation only fit for broken soldiers, when their feeble hands exchange rusty sword for belated mattock. Suppose you achieve your end ; suppose that vineyard upon vineyard foams with purple juice, while piled granaries collapse under endless mounds of grain ; suppose plump neatherds drive the crowding cows with their swollen udders into the reeking yards to milk : what then ? What use will it be to have enlarged your patrimony by sordid gains like these, to have lived recluse not only among such things, but, O deeper shame ! for such things' sake ? You will have only yourself to thank if one day you stand, you a nobleman born, obscure in your white hairs behind your juniors seated in debate, if you smart under the speech of some poor man risen to honour by office, and with anguish see yourself distanced by those in whom it would once have been presumption to follow in our train.
