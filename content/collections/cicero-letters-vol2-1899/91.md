In this you are not making yourself very conspicuous, for you seem to me to have your hands tied—by being at once a good citizen and a good friend. On quitting my province, I have put my quæstor Cælius in command. \"A mere boy,\" say you. Yes, but a quæstor, a young man of high rank, and in accordance with nearly universal precedent : for there was no one who had held higher office for me to put in that position. Pomptinus had departed long ago : my brother Quintus could not be induced : moreover, if I had left him, enemies would have said that I had not really left the province at the end of a year, in accordance with the decree of the senate, since I left a second self behind me.
