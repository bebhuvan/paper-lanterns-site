However, Manius Lepidus, L. Torquatus, Gaius Cassius, who are staying with me at Formiæ, are quite restored to life by Philotimus's letter. I, however, am afraid that the truer account is this: that we are all now practically captives; that Pompey is on the point of abandoning Italy, of whom, indeed (what a bitter pill to swallow!), Cæsar is said to be actually in pursuit.
