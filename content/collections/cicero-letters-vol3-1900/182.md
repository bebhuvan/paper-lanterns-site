Accordingly, I have received on the 10th your letter with its dearth of news. For what was there for you to send? To me however that was not unpleasing, whatever it contained, even if I learnt nothing else but that you had nothing to tell me. Yet, after all, you did say something—about Clodia. Where then is she, and when does she arrive? I like her property so much, that I put it next to Otho's above all others. But pray let us exert ourselves to hit upon some way of obtaining what I desire.
