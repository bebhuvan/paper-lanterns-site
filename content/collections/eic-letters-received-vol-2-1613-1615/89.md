So I returned back, but sent Simon our juribasso to you with a dozen of bread to be parted betwixt you and Captain Adams. I make John Phebe did deliver you the two fishes and letter I wrote you yesterday. He is now grown stately and will not serve in the English house for comprador; so he and I are parted. God speed him well etc. If the wind be out of the way, I would be glad of your company with Signor Ed. Sayer and Signor John Ocanano.
