Some four days since I received a letter from him wherein he wrote me that Mr. Peacock was upon departure for Langasaka and so for Cochin China. God send him a prosperous voyage. Also he wrote me that the Captain Chenesa and his new wife were not friends, and how that he had given her his wacadash or little cattan to cut off her little finger, the which she had done had she not been prevented per Maria, Harnando's woman, who paid dear for it, having her left thumb almost cut off. Some galls by the bearer hereof, who is the King's secretary of Ferando, I have sent you.
