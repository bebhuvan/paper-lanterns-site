You shall understand that of late here is much of that sort of indigo you have there about you come hither, which is called Jambusy (Jambusar), with some of Jengulie, which I conceive will be had here at most for 20 mamoodies the maund and the seer of 18 pisas, but it is such stuff that I dare not meddle with it at any rate, being, I doubt me, of a sort that Mr. Dodsworth told me by chance amongst the rest of the indigo they had in the Red Sea a fardle or 3 or 4 thereof was mingled, which they could not sell to any reasonable returning in England.
