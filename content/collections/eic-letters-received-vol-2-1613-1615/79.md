      "t": "MR. WICKHAM, Your letter with the paper in Japan characters I have received, and am heartily glad the leaks prove otherwise than was expected. Also I have spoken to Mr. Nealson and received your scissors of him, and send them along to you per the bearer hereof, John Phebe. And for John Ocanano the Spaniard, I know not what other course you should take with him but set him ashore at Syam, for as I understand him that was his desire. Only if any English ship be there to carry him to Bantam, I know you will favour him in what you may ; or else send him to Patania, if shipping be there to carry him. I for my part have not been forgetful to give him something out of my poverty. If you or others do the like, no doubt God will reward you, etc. I verily think now that you will have a fair wind, and therefore the Lord prosper you in your affairs and send you a good voyage and safely to return.
