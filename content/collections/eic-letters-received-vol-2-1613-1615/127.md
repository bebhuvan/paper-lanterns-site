The Hope being the first that began the fight, being at once laid aboard per the three Portingal ships and as many Portingal frigates as could lie about her, having entered their men into her and twice got their forecastle (?) but they very lustily shipped (?) them off again till such time as the other ships came and rescued her, else they would have put her in great danger. The Portingals came running aboard with great resolution, not so much as shooting a shot, but their courages were soon quailed. There were in their three ships many cavaliers, the most part whereof were most miserably burned and drowned ; so is the [report ?] they lost in all between four and five hundred men.
