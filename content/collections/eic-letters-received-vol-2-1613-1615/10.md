I much commend your diligence in learning out what you have done, and advising so largely thereof. Yesterday I received a letter from Tushma (Tsushima) from Ed. Sayer dated the 22nd ultimo. He writes that he hath sold but for 31 taels of cloth of Cambaia with 5 peculs pepper, and that the King and another man will take some 24 yards of broadcloth as he thinketh. He is out of hope of any good to be done there or Corea, and very desirous to go from thence for Focaty (Fukuoka), per means of the persuasions of a great merchant of that place who is now at Tushma. Mr. Peacock is now ready to set towards Cochin China. Captain Brower came from Langasaque two days past, having made ready all matters to send Adrian and Cornelius with a juribasso for that place.
