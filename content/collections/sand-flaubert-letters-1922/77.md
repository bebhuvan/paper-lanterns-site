We amuse ourselves madly at home so as to forget that we are prisoners, and I am prolonging my holidays in a ridiculous fashion. Not an iota of work from morning till night. What luck if you could say as much !—But what a fine winter, don’t you think so? Isn’t it lovely, the moonlight on the trees covered with snow?
