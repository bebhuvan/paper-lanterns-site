I had forgotten the custom of putting Prophets in the Stocks, and was obliged to have recourse to the Concordance to discover Jer. 29.20 for your Text, and found at the same time Jer. 20.23 that Jeremiah himself had been put in the Stocks. It may be thought impiety by many, but I could not help wishing that the ancient practice had been continued down to more modern times and that all the Prophets at least from Peter the Hermit, to Nimrod Hews inclusively had been confined in the Stocks and been prevented from spreading so many delusions and shedding so much blood.
