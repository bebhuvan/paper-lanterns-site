My garden is a little knoll with faces under it, and only the pines sing tunes, now the birds are absent. I cannot walk to the distant friends on nights piercing as these, so I put both hands on the window-pane, and try to think how birds fly, and imitate, and fail, like Mr ‘Rasselas.’ I could make a balloon of a dandelion, but the fields are gone, and only ‘Professor Lowe’ remains to weep with me.
