He brings us mamma's kind letter to her Adèle, which we read together, and a very cordial letter from Victor Foucher, which gave us also much pleasure. We expected to receive the cross of the Legion of Honor, and the papers, etc., as well, which you had told us would arrive at the beginning of the week. We have been disappointed in this, and my father would be glad if you would have the kindness to call once more at the office of the Legion to hasten their dispatch.
