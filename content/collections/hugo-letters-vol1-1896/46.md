What, you are left alone, alone in your isolation! They are inattentive and cold to my beloved Adèle, and this in my father's house! I am not angry, darling; I am grieved, deeply grieved. Knowing as I do your sweet temper and my father's unbounded kindness, I am astounded at what is going on. You have the right not only to kindness and attention, but to paternal tenderness and solicitude, to more perhaps than you receive from me. My poor dear father! if he could but look into my heart at this moment, he would see what intense pain is mingling with my infinite devotion to him and my deep love for you. I shall write to him as soon as I have time, but do not be uneasy; my letter will be skillful enough to avoid wounding his feelings, and yet make him see it all. There!
