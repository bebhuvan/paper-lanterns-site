Your application arrived too late; and for some time past the Duke of Angoulème had reserved the Inspector-Generalships for General Officers of the army in Spain. I do not know, dear papa, if this is a real misfortune; it is not a slight on your long and glorious services, as there can be no doubt that your claim would have been successful if there had been any competition; but the appointments were already placed at the Prince's disposal.
