Nor do I suppose you are to blame for this delay, but whatever has caused it, I am much distressed. Just imagine with what impatience I look forward to a letter from you in my loneliness, and what a blank I feel when I have been to the post in vain. All the pleasure of the day is at an end for me ; I have but one comfort left, and that is to read your dear letters over and over again, and kiss them a thousand times. I have not the courage to tell you about the Cathedral, and what I have admired or criticised in it. Good-by for to-day, my beloved ! My letter would be too sad, and you are quite sad enough already ! I will write more to-morrow. I shall then be nearer to receiving a letter from you, and therefore less miserable."
