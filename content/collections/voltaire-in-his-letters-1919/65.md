Just fancy that for more than a thousand years no traveller able to gain and give information had reached Constantinople through the countries which Lady Montagu traversed: she has seen the native land of Orpheus and Alexander: she has dined tête-à-tête with the widow of the Emperor Mustapha; she has translated Turkish songs and declarations of love, which are quite in the style of the Song of Songs; she has observed manners resembling those described by Homer, and has travelled with her Homer in her hand.
