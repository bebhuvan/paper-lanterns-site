I allude only to the arts whose glory you seek to perpetuate. They were either ignored or very ill carried on—beginning with the art of war. It was waged for forty years without a single man gaining the reputation of a clever general—without one whom posterity can compare with a Prince of Parma or a Prince of Orange.
