Poor as I may be in subject matter yet a daughter, and your daughter, is surely quite enough. She looked very well and I was very glad to see her so contented, happy and cheerful. When she first came to this country I was very much frightened, least the experiment should fail, for that would have been no trifle; but now all anxiety of that kind is over.
