The swedish philosopher is much inclined to adopt the views I have taken of the subject and thinks it highly probable that there exists an electrolytic body being composed of ozone and hydrogen and invariably associated with water just in the same way as, according to the most recent results of Mr. B., chloride of sodium is always found to be accompanied by small traces of bromide and Jodide of Sodium.
