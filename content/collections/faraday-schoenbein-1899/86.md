Both are beautiful. — As soon as I reach home I shall begin to prepare for ozone, making and repeating your experiments. This morning I hung out at my window one of the Ozonometer slips. That was about two hours ago. — Now when I moisten it, a tint of blue comes out between Nos 4 and 5 of the scale.
