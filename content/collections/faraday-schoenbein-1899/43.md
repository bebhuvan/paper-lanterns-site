I will tell you a case. A dear friend, a foreigner, now dead, sent me a M. I paid for the printing and did not receive one farthing back from the sale. I could not tell my friend this; he never asked for or had an account, and the thought often comes back to my mind that up to the day of his death, he might perhaps imagine I had made a profit by his work and never rendered him an account. — So much for my judgment in these matters.
