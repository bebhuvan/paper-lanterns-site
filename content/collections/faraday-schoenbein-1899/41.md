      "t": "As to that part of the “German Philosopher’s Work” which refers to the amiable philosophical inhabitant of the Royal Institution, I must beg to be allowed to differ widely from you and am bold enough, as to say that to my opinion the german writer knows, in some respects at least, the british philosopher much better, nay infinitely better, than you do. Having to write about some other things I cannot give you the reasons which make me hold such an opinion; one of them I shall however mention. As you are a Philosopher yourself, you must be well aware that objects being placed too near to the eye cannot be distincly seen by that organ. It is your case, my dear friend, you have seen the man of whom I am speaking at a distance which is too small, as to allow you to see him well, and so distinctly, as our german did, who was more favo[u]rably placed than you are. If you think that the book in question would be relished by british readers we could perhaps manage here a translation of it, as some english persons capable of doing such a work are living at Bâle."
