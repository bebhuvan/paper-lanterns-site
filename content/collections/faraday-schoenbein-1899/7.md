The instrument I made use of in my experiments, though indicating rather weak currents, does certainly not possess the highest degree of sensibility possible; (it contains about 100 coils) but as in a scientific point of view it is of very great importance to know, whether the peculiar condition of iron can in any way be called forth without exciting at the same time a current, I beg you to decide this question by means of your most delicate galvanometer.
