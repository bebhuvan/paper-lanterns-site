Formerly it was a real treat to me to write you a letter, now I have to make the greatest effort to take up my pen, and fulfill even the most urgent duties of a correspondent and it is hardly necessary to tell you the cause of that change. My mind is no more what it was a short time ago; its former cheerfulness is gone and melancholy feelings and sadness have taken possession of it, weighing the more heavily upon me, that Mrs. Schœnbein is very far from being comforted and consoled about our grievous loss.
