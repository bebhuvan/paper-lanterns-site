In the first place I have got there many friends of a very motley description, artists, poets, philosophers etc. and there is even a Nimrod found amongst them. Varietas delectat. It is indeed a great treat to me now and then to shake off from my shoulders the dust of the laboratory and store up my mind with the Images of exquisitely beautiful objects, creations certainly belonging to an order of things infinitely superior to that under which we range physical phenomena and philosophical truths.
