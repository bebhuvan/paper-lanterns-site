He is not one of those whom I inherited, or one of those who have in any other way been brought up with me, for all such have received the education of freemen. I have treated them almost as my equals, and I may add that they love me as their chosen chief, rather than fear me as their lawful master. Philoromus, that is the name of the runaway, was a slave belonging to my niece, the daughter of Amelius, and became mine through her.
