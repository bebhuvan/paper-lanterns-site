For this present night Robert Burges, having been all this afternoon forth of the ship, at nine of the clock came aboard very drunk, having abused himself in running after the Javas women, and fighting and wrangling with the Chinas, the which the King gave us warning at our first coming to him ; and at his coming aboard abused us all and fell together by the ears and made an uproar in the ship that all the town wondered at us, which for to avoid hereafter we do as we have done. In his place Mathew will serve. I pray you supply our wants with some other men and we will make a shift. At your coming up you shall hear at large of his mad pranks. For this we have done we will answer.
