His answers were cross and shuffling, not resolving of any good course, but to proceed as they had begun, to abuse us by their force. On the next day our boat set me on land with one man with me, to see if I could speak with the Arankayos or relieve that one Englishman that was alone in the castle. As soon as I was on land, one Fleming more forward than wise comes after me and steps before me with his truncheon over my head, peremptorily commanding me to come before his Captain; his actions being insufferable that briefly we fell to the matter. Presently in the meantime comes running the Captain and thirty small shot, some crying “ Shoot him,” some “ Kill him,” and others “ Put him in the bilboes.” The Captain told me it is death for any of the English to come there on land.
