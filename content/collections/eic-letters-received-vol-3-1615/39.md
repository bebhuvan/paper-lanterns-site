Put enough to, for you may take away when you will, and it stands us upon to stand out against the Spaniards in this action. You may lawfully say that the King of Spain usurpeth Portingall by force and keepeth the rightful heirs out, as he doth the like in other parts of the world and would do the like in Japon if he could, and the padres are fit instruments to stir people to rebellion.
