He put not into China, as was reported, but into the island called Leque Grande, where he was indifferently entreated, but could not be suffered to repair his junk, as he desired, being forced only to stay for the monsoone to bring him back again hither."

Either, I say, we must procure a peaceable trade in China, or else, as the Hollanders do, to trade with them per force.
