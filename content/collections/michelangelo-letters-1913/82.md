      "t": ",—Last night our friend the Capitano Cuio [Dini] and certain other gentlemen were kind enough to invite me to supper with them. It gave me very great pleasure, because it withdrew me somewhat from my melancholy, or rather, folly ; and not only did I enjoy the supper, which was exceedingly good, but I enjoyed the conversation which adorned it still more. My delight in their talk was increased still further when I heard the said Capitano Cuio mention your name : nor was this all, for it further rejoiced me beyond measure when I heard the Capitano say that in art you were without an equal, and that you were so considered in Rome. If it had been possible to experience greater pleasure, that pleasure would have been mine. So you see that my judgment has not turned out to be wrong ; therefore do not deny that you are peerless when I address you as such, for I have too many witnesses, and—God be praised for it—there is a picture of yours here which wins over to my opinion everyone who has any intelligence."
