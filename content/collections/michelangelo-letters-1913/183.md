He leaves me in affliction and distress so great that it would have been better for me if I had died too, such was the love I bore him. Nor did he deserve less of me, for he had grown to be a man of worth, full of loyalty and faithful service ; wherefore I feel as though his death had deprived me also of life, and I can find no rest. I should accordingly be glad to see thee, though I do not know whether thou couldst leave Florence, for love of thy wife. Let me know if thou art able to come as far as Rome within the next month or six weeks, presuming always that the Duke [Cosimo] will give thee permission.
