However, my answer is that I have given no promises here, so that if thou likewise hast given no promises in Florence I think it would be best to let the matter drop if thou art certain that she is short-sighted ; for as thou sayest, it is a defect that goes by heredity. Once more I would impress upon thee what I have already written on many occasions : thou art to seek out someone who is healthy, more for the love of God than to secure a dowry, provided that she be a good woman and of noble birth. Do not let it trouble thee if she be poor, for then she will bring thee the more tranquillity. On this head I have no more to say. I am an old man and have but little capital, and even that I do not wish to spend here. Therefore, when thou hearest of some house or property which would be a sound investment for the sum of 1,500 crowns, I shall be ready to buy it. Give the matter thine attention, therefore, so that if I were to die here—as may happen any day—the money may not be lost to thee."
