      "t": ",—With regard to thy marriage : I have just heard from a friend that the defect which caused thee to give up all thought of the damsel I wrote about does not exist, that is to say, she is not short-sighted. He declares it was a friend of thine who told thee this because he wanted thee to marry some other person he had in view, but that she was not as yet of marriageable age, and therefore he invented this story so as to make thee delay until she was ready for thee. It seems to me that thou shouldst take her, if it is really true that there is no defect in her vision. Have a care that thou be not led by the nose by people who are of inferior rank to thee. I have nothing more to add on this head.
