And now, being an old man, as thou knowest well, I should like to do something for the welfare of my soul—to bestow something in charity, that is to say; for I can perform no other pious act that I know of. I desire, therefore, to have a certain sum paid over to thee in Florence, which thou art to pay out, or rather distribute, in alms wherever the need is greatest. This sum will amount to about three hundred crowns. I have asked Bandini about it: that is, I have asked him to have it paid out in Florence for me, and he replied that within the next four months he would deliver it in person. I do not wish to wait so long, so that if thou hast some Florentine friend to whom I could give the money and be sure that it would reach thee, let me know, and I will adopt this course.
