I will explain the reason to you when I have more time: suffice it that on Wednesday morning I went back again and he caused me to be paid four hundred gold ducats of the Camera. Of this sum I am sending three hundred broad ducats to Florence, for I am paying three hundred broad ducats of gold to the Altoviti here so that the Strozzi may pay you the money there.
