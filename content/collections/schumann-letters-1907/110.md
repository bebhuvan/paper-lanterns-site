      "t": "You must now be well in the middle of my symphony. Do you still remember the first rehearsal of it in the year 1841—and the 'stopped' trumpets and horns at the beginning ? It sounded as if the orchestra had a cold in its head ; I can't help laughing when I think of it. And now let me thank you for again thinking about my piece and again taking trouble over it. It is with the greatest pleasure that I think of that first evening's performance. How beautifully it went, better than I have ever heard it since ! I might perhaps repeat the experience to-morrow, but I dare not come. I am sorry to say that I have not yet regained my full strength ; every divergence from my simple régime upsets me, and induces morbid irritability. That is why I reluctantly stayed away when my wife was with you. I must avoid every form of gaiety. There is nothing for it but to go on hoping, and that I am determined to do."
