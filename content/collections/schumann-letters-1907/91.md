      "t": "I must apologize for troubling you again, but the honour and happiness of two people are at stake—two people who really deserve to be united after the sufferings they have endured. I feel I must discuss the matter with you again, going over each point in detail. Please fix a time when I can see you. If you should have the smallest doubt of our ultimate success, do not hide it from me. Clara would be beside herself if this public attempt should fail, and how shall I describe my own feelings in such an event! Therefore, if you are doubtful, we must seek another solution, though I confess I know of none at present. If, on the other hand, you feel sure of our success, you must reassure me, and help my heroic girl with the devotion she deserves."
