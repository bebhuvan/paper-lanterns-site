Emilia List, with whom Clara was staying, wrote in the same strain, pointing out that Clara could not follow her artistic career unless she was rich enough to be independent of household cares. Emilia even proposed that Schumann should take over the book-shop of his brother Eduard as the readiest means of procuring the required income. These letters crossed with Schumann's enthusiastic epistle of May 4, and were followed by another still more discouraging letter from Clara, which Schumann immediately destroyed.
