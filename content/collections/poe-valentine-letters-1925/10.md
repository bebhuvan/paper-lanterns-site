On that day Edgar was “present for duty” with his battery at Old Point, but the death of Frances Allan evidently caused her husband to relent and ask for a week’s leave for the young sergeant major who had been her only child, and of course he must have furnished means for the journey. Again proof is furnished by a combination of the Valentine Museum letters and the records in the Library of Congress.
