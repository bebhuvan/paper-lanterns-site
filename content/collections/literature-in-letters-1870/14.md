The next Sunday the man followed him to church, and got into the next pew; he leaned over, and said, “My money; give me my money!” My lord went to the end of the pew; the man too. “Give me my money!” The sermon was on avarice; and the text, “Cursed are they that heap up riches.” The man groaned out, “O Lord!” and pointed to my Lord Bath.
