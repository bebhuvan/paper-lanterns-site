The greatest comfort I had was an intention (which I would have made practicable) to have attended you in your journey, to which I had brought that person to consent who only could have hindered me, by a tie which, though it may be more tender, I do not think more strong than that of friendship. But I fear there will be no way left me to tell you this great truth, that I remember you, that I love you, that I am grateful to you, that I entirely esteem and value you ; no way but that one which needs no open warrant to authorize it, or secret conveyance to secure it ; which no bills can preclude and no Kings prevent ; a way that can reach to any part of the world where you may be ; where the very whisper, or even the wish, of a friend must not be heard, or even suspected.
