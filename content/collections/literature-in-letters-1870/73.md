      "t": "I never see any Boston newspapers. You mention there being often something in them to do me honor. I am obliged to them. On the other hand, some of our papers here are endeavoring to disgrace me. I have long been accustomed to receive more blame, as well as more praise, than I deserved. 'Tis the lot of every public man, and I have one account to balance the other. As you observe, there was no d——n your souls in the story of the poker when I told it. The late dresser of it was probably the same, or perhaps of kin to him, who, in relating a dispute that happened between Queen Anne and the Archbishop of Canterbury, concerning a vacant mitre, which the Queen was for bestowing on a person the Archbishop thought unworthy, made both the Queen and the Archbishop swear three or four thumping oaths in every sentence of the discussion; and the Archbishop at last gained his point.
