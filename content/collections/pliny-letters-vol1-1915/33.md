Caius Septicius, his uncle, is the most plain, sincere, candid, and trusty man I ever knew. There is a rivalry amongst them who shall show me most affection; which nevertheless they all give me in an equal degree. I have now an opportunity of repaying my debt of gratitude to the whole family, in the single person of Sextus. Accordingly, I warmly solicit my friends, I entreat, I make house-to-house visits, I perambulate the places of public resort, and put my whole influence and popularity to the touch, by petitions on his behalf. I must beg of you likewise to condescend to take some share of this trouble with me; I will return you the same good office whenever you shall require it, and even without your request.
