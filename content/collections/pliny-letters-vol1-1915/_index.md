  "Covering Books I–VI of the correspondence of Gaius Plinius Caecilius Secundus (Pliny the Younger, A.D. 61/62–c. 113; scan leaf 17, p. xi), Volume I of this two-volume edition presents 150 letters spanning Rome under Nerva and Trajan: senatorial trials such as the prosecution of Marius Priscus (Book II. xi, scan leaves 145–157), literary circles, villas in Tuscany and Laurentum, domestic life, and the two celebrated eyewitness letters to Cornelius Tacitus on the eruption of Mount Vesuvius and the death of the elder Pliny (Book VI. xvi, scan leaves 497–505, and VI. xx, scan leaves 511–519).",
  "William Melmoth's classic eighteenth-century English translation (first published 1746) was revised by classical scholar Winifred Margaret Lambart Hutchinson (1868–1937) and first printed in 1915 (prepared here from the unmodified 1931 impression of the 1915 edition; title and imprint leaves, scan leaves 9–10). Volume I includes the Preface (pp. v–vii, scan leaves 11–13), Table of Contents (p. ix, scan leaf 15), Hutchinson's Introduction on Pliny's life, letters, and manuscripts (pp. xi–xvi, scan leaves 17, 20), and the English translations of Books I–VI (scan leaves 23–557 odd). This Paper Lanterns edition preserves Melmoth and Hutchinson's historical orthography, small-caps openings, textual footnotes, and epistolary headers verbatim.",
