In good earnest, I am tired with repeating over and over the same compliments, “How d’ye do ?” and “ I hope you are well.” Why should our letters, too, for ever turn upon petty domestic concerns ? It is true, indeed, the direction of the public weal is in the hands of a single person, who, for the general good, takes upon himself solely to ease us of the care and weight of government ; but still that bountiful source of power permits, by a very wholesome dispensation, some streams to flow down to us : and these we may not only imbibe ourselves, but, as it were, administer them by letter to our absent friends.
