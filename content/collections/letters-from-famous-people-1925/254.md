You can get more solid satisfaction out of it every day in the week, fifty-two weeks in the year, than from anything else. Nothing is so fine as a job that you love—and you haven't the moral right to take one that you don't like, since nowadays there are so many tasks to choose from, even for the girls. American girls are extraordinarily lucky, for they don't have to sit around and wait for some man, anybody, to marry, but they can do as good work as a man and be independent.
