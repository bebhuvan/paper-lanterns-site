      "t": "I HAVE your letter of May 4, asking me for a message for your students, and of course I should be glad if I could be of any service either to you or to them. They are being trained for very important work in the world in thus preparing under your supervision for business, which plays so large a part in our modern life. The training of such quality cannot but be of advantage, not only to them, but to the business in which they engage. I venture to express the hope that while they are carrying out the work for which they are especially educated, they will always remember that one duty which should be uppermost in the minds of all young men and women acquiring an education, and that is, that in their life work they must never forget that their first duty is to their country and that the business of the country ought to take precedence over all other and be helped and improved by them to the extent of their opportunities and their ability."
