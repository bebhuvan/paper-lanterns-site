Of course the real difficulty is that you asked me to preach a little sermon to the eight people who doubtless are now graduated and gone. I have been greatly interested in those eight people ever since I began to teach at Harvard in 1883. I suppose they are the same people; some wear trousers and some wear petticoats, but they are all alike to me—and all the same age. That, you know, is the fountain of perpetual youth for the teacher.
