In this relation I often think of a certain kind of moss which I very frequently encounter in my journeys of exploration in the far north. This moss appears first as a single filament as fine as a woman's hair, and grows from a healthy young spruce or cedar. As time passes, these hair-like filaments of moss increase in number, until they appear to be very beautiful.
