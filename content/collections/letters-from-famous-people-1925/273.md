It makes little difference what his fad is. It may be in art, science, or letters. Sir John Hubbock was known as the busiest man in London. He was actively engaged in scores of the most important business enterprises, to all of which he gave close attention. But as a recreation he devoted much time to scientific investigation and wrote books that have been a delight and an inspiration to thousands. No man of his time got more out of life than he.
