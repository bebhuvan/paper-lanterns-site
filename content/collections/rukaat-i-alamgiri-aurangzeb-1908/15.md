Bernier calls him ‘ the most accomplished statesman of Asia ’. He took part with the prince Aurungzebe in the seige of Balkh (1646) and of Kandahar (1648-1651). Shah Jehan sent him to Chitor in order to demolish its fort and to punish the Ráná, Jagat Singh, the father of Raja Jai Singh (1653). He was murdered at the instigation of Dara in 1655. He was also a good scholar ; hence he was called ‘Allámi’ (learned). He was the patron of Abdūl Hamid Láhori, the author of ‘Bádshàh Námeh’, a history of the first twenty years of Shah Jehan’s reign.
