In 1689 he quelled a mutiny at Ahmedabad. For this his firmness Aurungzebe honoured him with the title of Shuja’at Khan. He was, before this, governor of Ajmere and Jodhpur. After his death in 1703 Prince A’azam was made governor of Ahmedabad. The Khan was one of the ablest of Gujarat viceroys and was very honest. Aurungzebe had great trust in him and throughout his services he was never found guilty by him. From a lower position he raised himself to a very high rank.
