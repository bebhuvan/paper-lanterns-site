Khan Jehan Bahadur Zafar Jang Kokaltash, a foster-brother of Aurungzebe and a great nobleman in the imperial court, his original name being Mir Malik Husain. In 1672 he was sent to the Deccan as governor. He began to rebuild the fortress of Surat against Shivaji. He was sent against Abul Hasan, last king of Golconda. In 1674 Aurungzebe raised him from the rank of 700 horse to that of 7000 and conferred upon him the title of Khan Jehan Bahadur Zafar Jang. In 1691 he was appointed governor of Allahabad. In the same year he was made governor of the Punjab. He died in 1697. Aurungzebe was greatly offended with him for his inactivity and failure in war. He took part in the Deccan War. He seems to be the author of ‘Tarikh-i-Assam’ (History of Assam). ‘He did not like much talking.’ He often received bribes from the Mahrattas. Cf. Let. XCII, CXXVII and CXXXIV. For A’akel Khan, Cf.
